About Edmonton Gallery Walk

Walk into the working heart of Edmonton's vibrant gallery district: six independent small businesses, one street, and decades of exhibitions, openings, and discoveries. Join us for the Edmonton Gallery Walk on Saturday, September 19 and Sunday, September 20, along 124 Street from 101 Avenue to 107 Avenue. Every gallery you'll visit is licensed by the City of Edmonton and represents its own roster of professional artists, many for decades. Discover original art in a range of mediums, from paintings and blown glass to sculpture, all available for purchase directly from the gallery.

Organized by the directors of dedicated art galleries since the very first walk, this curated experience is built on decades of trust among galleries, artists, and the public. Find our member galleries, hours, and event details at EdmontonGalleryWalk.com.
